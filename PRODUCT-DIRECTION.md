# Fuel-stop decision explainer

## Current evidence

Route sampling and rule-based fuel planning exist.

## Next product increment — planned

Show why each stop was selected and where range or station data makes a route infeasible.

## Acceptance gate

An infeasible route returns an explicit gap rather than a deceptively complete itinerary.

A release also needs reproducible checks, useful empty/error states, keyboard/mobile review where applicable, and an inspectable example with appropriate data. Planned work above is not shipped functionality.
